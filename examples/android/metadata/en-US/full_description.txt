DevPulse checks whether the Android apps you already installed still have a heartbeat.

It looks at Google Play public update dates, the official F-Droid index plus extra repos such as IzzyOnDroid, and public forges. Then it surfaces software that looks stale or abandoned so you can find a living replacement, another sideload source, or a repo worth forking.

The app is completely local. No accounts, analytics, or telemetry. Network is used only when you scan or opt into later lookups. Distributed via GitHub Releases only (not listed on F-Droid). License: GPL-3.0-or-later. applicationId: app.devpulse.

DevPulse does not auto-install other apps and does not claim an app is safe or malware. Staleness is not a security audit.
